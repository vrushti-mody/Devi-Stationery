Returns &
refunds.

This website does not currently sell items online or accept payment. The product pages are a catalogue-style guide and do not create a purchase. For an item bought at the counter, speak to the store with your receipt so the team can review the item and explain the available options.

Printed and customised work

For print, copy, lamination, binding, or scanning jobs, confirm the file, quantity, format, finish, price, and expected completion time with the store before work begins. If there is a problem with completed work, contact the store promptly and bring the receipt or job details so it can be reviewed.
