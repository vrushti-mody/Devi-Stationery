Your privacy,
plain and simple.

This policy explains what this website stores and what happens when you contact Devi Stationery and Xerox Centre in person or by phone. This site is an informational storefront and quote-list demo; online checkout, customer accounts, analytics tags, and marketing pixels are not enabled.

Information stored on this device

The site stores your quote bag and cookie preference choice in your browser’s local storage so they remain available when you return. This information stays in the browser and is not sent to the shop by this site. You can clear it using your browser settings; clear the quote bag from the bag panel; or change optional cookie preferences from the footer.

Cookies and optional technologies

This starter site does not set analytics or advertising cookies or load analytics or marketing scripts. The preferences panel lets you record a choice for essential, analytics, and marketing storage. Essential local storage supports the bag and preference choice. Optional analytics and marketing remain inactive and no third-party trackers load, even if a preference is selected, until a provider is deliberately added.

When you call or visit

If you call or visit the store, the shop may receive information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, print instructions, or order enquiry. This website does not collect or transmit that information. Ask the shop directly how it handles information you provide by phone or at the counter.

Third-party links

The location and review links open Google Maps. When you visit Google, Google may process information under its own privacy policy and settings. This website does not embed Google Maps or load review content from Google automatically.

Your choices and requests

You can withdraw or change your optional preferences at any time using “Cookie preferences” in the website footer. Since no optional tracking scripts currently run, this choice is stored only in your browser. For a question about information you shared directly with the shop, contact +91 85917 47983 or visit the store at Shop No P2, Hemant Manjrekar Market, Sion East, Mumbai 400037.

Security and updates

The site should be hosted over HTTPS. No payment card information is requested or handled here. We may update this policy when site features or store practices change; the date above will be revised to reflect material changes.

This policy describes the current starter website’s behaviour. The store should review it against its actual business practices and applicable privacy obligations before publication.
